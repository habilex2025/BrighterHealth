The challenge

Private hospitals can't afford to be
invisible.invisible.

Australia's private hospitals deliver the majority of the nation's planned surgery, yet the sector is under more pressure than ever.

Margins are tight, insurers are squeezing, and patients — with rising out-of-pocket costs and a public alternative — are questioning every decision.

In this environment, marketing isn't a luxury. It's how hospitals protect referrals, fill theatre lists, attract the specialists and nurses they need, and remind their communities why they matter.

As a healthcare marketing agency working across Australia, we partner with hospital boards, CEOs and marketing teams who want a team that already speaks their language.

FAQs

Private hospital marketing, answered.

Do you only work with South Australian hospitals?

No — we're based in Adelaide but work with healthcare organisations across Australia.

What does a private hospital marketing agency do?

We connect hospital strategy to practical delivery across brand, patient communications, websites, digital campaigns, media and specialist support.

We have an internal marketing team. How do you fit in?

We work as an extension of internal teams — providing strategy, creative firepower and media buying capability, the way we do with Burnside Hospital's communications team.

How can marketing increase private hospital admissions?

Effective hospital marketing builds awareness, makes services and specialists easier to find, and reduces friction between referral, research and booking.

Is your work AHPRA and TGA compliant?

Yes. Every campaign is reviewed against AHPRA advertising guidelines and the Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code before it goes live.

Where do we start?

Most hospitals begin with a marketing audit — an independent review of your brand, digital presence and referral pathways, delivered with recommendations your board can act on.

What makes healthcare marketing different from general marketing?

Healthcare decisions involve trust, clinical complexity, regulation, referrers and vulnerable audiences. Effective work must be accurate, accessible and useful while still giving people a clear reason to choose or recommend the hospital.

How do you market a private hospital to patients without undermining referrers?

Patient and referrer marketing should work together. We clarify the role of GPs and specialists, provide useful service information and create pathways that help patients act on a referral rather than replace clinical advice.

How should a private hospital market to GPs and referrers?

Effective referrer marketing combines accurate service information, specialist access, useful clinical updates, responsive communications and consistent relationship support rather than relying on occasional newsletters.

Can you market individual hospital service lines?

Yes. We can build campaigns around priorities such as orthopaedics, cardiac care, maternity, rehabilitation, oncology or day surgery, with messaging and media matched to the audience and referral pathway.

How do you support visiting medical officers and specialist referrals?

We help hospitals package VMO support, create specialist profiles and content, improve find-a-specialist journeys, and run compliant campaigns that build awareness of both the specialist and hospital.

Can you help launch a new hospital service or facility?

Yes. We plan the positioning, stakeholder communications, launch campaign, referral materials, digital content and media needed to introduce a new service, site or redevelopment.

What is included in a hospital marketing audit?

We review your positioning, communications, website, referral pathways, campaigns and market visibility, then prioritise the opportunities most likely to support hospital goals.

Can you help us run a pooled specialist campaign?

Yes. We developed this model with Burnside Hospital and manage the entire program: specialist engagement, packages, creative, media and reporting.

Can you improve our hospital website and digital marketing?

Yes. We design hospital websites around real patient journeys and support them with search, content and digital campaigns that reach people while they are choosing care.

How do you measure hospital marketing performance?

Reporting is tailored to the goal, combining measures such as reach, search visibility, enquiries, referral activity, bookings and admissions where data is available.

Can you help attract specialists, nurses and allied health staff?

Yes. We develop employer brands, recruitment campaigns and VMO support programs that communicate your culture, facilities and career opportunities clearly.

Can you support a hospital rebrand or redevelopment?

Yes. We can guide brand strategy, stakeholder communications and campaign delivery through new services, redevelopments, mergers and ownership transitions.

Do you create content for private hospitals?

Yes. We create service pages, specialist profiles, patient guides, referral materials, campaigns, video and social content, with clinical review and compliance built into the process.

Do you manage hospital media planning and buying?

Yes. We plan and buy across digital, search, social, outdoor, print, radio and television where appropriate, then report performance against the campaign objective.

Can you improve our Find a Specialist directory?

Yes. We review taxonomy, profiles, filters, referral information and calls to action so patients and referrers can find the right specialist and understand the next step.

Can you help explain hospital fees and patient pathways?

Yes. Clear information about referrals, admissions, preparation, likely costs and next steps reduces uncertainty and helps patients move from research to booking with confidence.

How do you approach SEO and generative search for private hospitals?

We organise hospital content around real patient and referrer questions, clear service and specialist information, accessible page structure, credible evidence and accurate local signals so search engines and AI systems can understand and reference it.

Can you work across multiple hospital campuses or health networks?

Yes. We can develop a shared strategy and brand system while tailoring service, audience and campaign activity for individual hospitals, regions or campuses.
